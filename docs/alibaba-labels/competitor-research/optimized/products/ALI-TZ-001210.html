详情页五条卖点

1

The tubular film shrinks around the cap and neck, covering the joint between them with one continuous band.

筒状膜受热后沿桶盖和颈口收拢，一圈覆盖盖颈接合处，形成完整的封口外观。

2

The band fits over an already capped jug, so the printed seal can be added without changing the cap design.

水桶旋盖或压盖后即可套膜，沿用现有桶盖也能增加印刷封口。

3

A cap band sized to the actual neck profile lets bottlers choose how far the seal extends below the cap rim.

膜套按实际盖径和颈部轮廓定制，封到盖沿下方多深可随桶型调整。

4

Wraparound printing makes a brand mark or opening instruction visible around the closure while leaving the jug body free for its main label.

环绕印刷可在桶口呈现品牌图案或开启提示，把桶身的展示空间留给主标签。

5

An optional perforated tear line gives customers a defined place to start removing the sleeve before opening the jug.

可选易撕线为消费者提供明确的起撕位置，开盖前更容易找到从哪里拆膜。

报价说明

瓶盖外径、瓶颈周长、成品高度、收缩设备；撕线及膜材按打样确认。MOQ、价格和交期按确认规格、数量及目的地报价。

八条产品FAQ

Can you size the band from the jug capacity alone?

Send the cap and neck dimensions. Jugs of the same capacity can use different caps.

Which dimensions should I send?

Send the maximum cap diameter, the neck circumference and the desired finished band height.

Do you supply the cap or jug?

The quote covers the cap bands. The jug and cap are for application reference.

Can the band have a tear line?

Mark the desired location on the artwork and request a perforated trial.

What should I test on the line?

Check fit and appearance with your actual cap and shrink equipment and check opening if perforation is requested.

What artwork should I send?

Send a vector PDF or editable artwork with the target dimensions and mark any special finish or functional area.

Can I review a proof before bulk production?

Request a digital proof or physical sample and include the intended application when confirming the sample plan.

How do I get a quotation?

Send your target quantity and destination, plus these details: cap diameter neck circumference and finished band height. Pricing and lead time depend on the approved specification.

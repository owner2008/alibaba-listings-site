详情页五条卖点

1

A die-cut paper face frames the brand emblem on each cigar, so the identity stays with the cigar after it leaves the presentation box.

模切纸环把品牌徽记留在每支雪茄上，从礼盒中单独取出时也能辨认品牌。

2

The band closes at the rear overlap, leaving the front emblem and its border uninterrupted when the cigar is on display.

搭口设在背面，正面徽记和边框不会被接缝截断，整支陈列时图案更完整。

3

Band length and face height can be tailored separately, allowing the same emblem to fit different cigar ring gauges without simply enlarging the entire design.

环绕长度和正面高度可分别定制，同一徽记适配不同环径时，无需把整张图稿等比放大。

4

Distinctive colours or blend names can be printed within a shared band design, helping buyers tell a collection apart in an assorted box.

同系列可共用纸环轮廓，用印色或配方名称区分款式，组合礼盒中的各支雪茄也容易辨认。

5

Optional foil accents or embossing can highlight the emblem on a presentation edition while the standard line uses the same printed layout.

礼盒版可选局部烫印或压纹突出徽记，常规版沿用同一印刷版式，系列识别保持一致。

八条产品FAQ

How should I measure the band?

Send the wrap circumference and the band width, or a flat sample with its overlap marked.

Does the order include cigars?

The quote covers printed paper bands. Any display form shown is for fitting reference.

Where should I place the logo?

Mark the intended front center on the artwork and keep key details clear of the overlap.

Can I request foil or embossing?

Identify the finish and its position for feasibility review and separate pricing.

Can I order multiple artwork versions?

Send a separate file and quantity for each version so the quotation can allocate them correctly.

What artwork should I send?

Send a vector PDF or editable artwork with the target dimensions and mark any special finish or functional area.

Can I review a proof before bulk production?

Request a digital proof or physical sample and include the intended application when confirming the sample plan.

How do I get a quotation?

Send your target quantity and destination, plus these details: flat width length and overlap by artwork. Pricing and lead time depend on the approved specification.

What is the product supplied as?

It is supplied as a bound book of printed self adhesive Halloween stickers, with release backing supporting the sticker designs.

What material is used for the stickers?

The sticker face material is clear PET film. The book cover and release backing are separate components from the PET sticker face.

What can I decorate with this sticker book?

Use the printed designs for journal pages, planners, scrapbooks, cards and DIY paper craft layouts.

How are the stickers applied?

Peel a sticker from its backing, position it on a clean, dry page and press it down. Separate glue is not required.

Can I customize the printed designs and cover?

Can the book size and number of sheets be customized?

Does one sheet mean one sticker?

No. A sticker sheet can contain several individual designs. State the number of books, sheets per book and sticker layout separately when ordering.

What information is needed for a quotation?

Send the book size, sheets per book, cover requirements, artwork, number of books and delivery destination. Pricing is based on the complete book configuration and order quantity.

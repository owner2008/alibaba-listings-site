What product is supplied?

We supply finished printed self adhesive label rolls for coffee flavor syrup bottles. The bottles, syrup, coffee beans and other scene props are application examples and are not included.

What material and supply format are used?

The label face is opaque white BOPP film with an adhesive backing and release liner. Finished labels are wound around a cardboard core for roll supply.

How do gold foil and raised spot UV differ?

Gold foil creates a thin metallic accent on selected artwork. Clear raised spot UV adds a low raised glossy coating to selected lettering. The illustrated tactile effect comes from UV buildup rather than mechanical embossing; the flower, beans and pods remain flat printed graphics.

Can I customize the logo and artwork?

Yes. Customize your logo, text, print colors, layout and die cut outline. Provide separate print, foil and raised UV areas in the artwork. The Labelnexor Vanilla artwork is an original sample design.

Which label dimensions can I choose?

Custom sizes are available for your bottle labeling area. The 50.8 by 76.2 mm and 76.2 by 101.6 mm image examples are market format examples, not measured bottle fits or fixed stock sizes. Illustrations are not to scale.

What roll specifications should I provide?

State the total number of individual labels, labels per roll, core diameter and winding direction. Core options include 3/4 in, 1 in and 3 in. These specifications are listed separately from the label size.

What are the minimum order and lead time?

MOQ from 500 pieces and lead time from 5 days. Pieces refer to individual labels rather than rolls. The order specification lists the production configuration and schedule.

What information is needed for a quotation?

Send the label width and height, total label quantity, white BOPP material requirement, artwork, foil and raised UV areas, and roll specifications. Production charges, freight and taxes are itemized separately.

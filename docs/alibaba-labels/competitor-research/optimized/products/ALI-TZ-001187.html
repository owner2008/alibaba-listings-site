What is supplied with this product?

Custom printed rectangular vinyl nameplate labels with adhesive backing on a continuous release liner roll. Television housings and other application props are not included.

What information can I print on the label?

Provide your logo, model, serial information, product description and instruction text. LABELNEXOR, LX TV32 and DEMO 0001 are original demonstration data. Use your approved final artwork for production.

Which label sizes are available?

Common rectangular examples are 76.2 x 50.8 mm and 101.6 x 50.8 mm, stated as width x height. Custom sizes are available. Measure the suitable flat rear panel area before choosing the finished size.

Is this a metal nameplate or an adhesive label?

This configuration is a thin printed vinyl adhesive label on release liner, with a white border. It is not a rigid metal plate. The illustrated configuration has a black printed face.

How should the labels be applied?

Apply to a suitable clean, dry, smooth exterior rear panel area. Peel from the liner, align and press the label into place. Evaluate a physical sample on the actual housing material and surface texture before bulk use.

Can the roll layout be customized?

Specify the core diameter, winding direction, label orientation, gap, roll layout and total label pieces. Core options are 3/4 in, 1 in and 3 in. Labels per roll are defined separately for the order.

What are the minimum order and production time?

MOQ from 100 individual label pieces. Send the artwork, dimensions, roll requirements and quantity to request the production schedule. The number of labels is different from the number of rolls.

What is needed for a quotation?

Send the finished dimensions, vinyl specification, artwork, number of designs, total label quantity, roll layout, intended housing surface and delivery destination. Product price, samples, tooling and shipping are listed separately.

What are swimwear hygienic liners gusset stickers and wave bottom stickers?

These terms refer to thin self adhesive fitting labels placed on the inner gusset of swimwear bikini bottoms or underwear. Swimwear hygienic liners and gusset stickers describe the product clearly. Wave bottom sticker is an additional listing keyword for the same use.

Are clear and opaque versions both available for custom orders?

Yes. Both clear and opaque versions can be customised. Clear vinyl and opaque kraft paper are separate material options with a printed adhesive face and release backing. The current image set shows opaque kraft paper examples. Select one material configuration for each quoted item.

Can I customise the print colours on both clear and opaque liners?

Yes. Send your logo artwork fitting instructions and colour references for either version. Rose sage lavender and burgundy in the images are opaque printed examples. The visual result depends on the selected face material and approved production artwork.

How should the liner be applied?

Peel the printed liner from its release backing align it along the inside garment gusset and press the face and edges flat. Assess the fit and adhesive compatibility using the actual garment fabric before bulk application.

What are the size examples in the images?

The size guide shows 110 x 50 mm and 137 x 50 mm width by height as manufacturer published market examples for swimwear hygiene liners. They are not measured dimensions of our photographed concept or a universal standard. Custom dimensions follow your garment and drawing.

Can the logo shape dimensions and fitting instructions be changed?

Yes. Both transparent and opaque liners support custom logos print designs die cut shapes dimensions and fitting instructions. Provide your garment requirements and approved artwork. LABELNEXOR the wave mark and wording shown are demonstration content.

How are these labels supplied and counted?

The illustrated supply is finished printed labels on release backing in sheets or narrow strips. Orders are counted per individual printed liner. Specify total label quantity liners per sheet or strip and packing details separately.

What are the minimum order lead time and quotation requirements?

MOQ starts from 500 individual labels and lead time starts from 5 days. Send the clear or opaque material choice liner dimensions shape artwork print colours total label quantity and backing layout for an itemised quotation and production schedule.

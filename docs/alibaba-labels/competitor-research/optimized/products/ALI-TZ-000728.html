八条产品FAQ

What comes in the set?

The set pairs decorative washi tape rolls with a matching sticker sheet. Confirm the roll count and sheet layout when ordering.

Can I supply my own repeat artwork?

Yes. Send your pattern at the intended tape width and mark where the repeat joins.

How should I mark the gold areas?

Identify the foil areas on a separate artwork layer so fine lines and solid areas can be reviewed at print size.

What dimensions do you need?

Specify the width and length per roll plus the finished sticker sheet dimensions.

Can the stickers match the tape motifs?

Yes. Identify the motifs you want on the sheet and show the intended cut outline for each sticker.

Which designs are shown?

The examples include crescents and stars, botanical sprigs, Art Deco fans and geometric repeats.

How do I check the foil appearance?

Review a physical sample under the lighting in which the set will be used. The apparent brightness changes with the viewing angle.

What information determines the quotation?

Send the artwork, roll dimensions, rolls per set, sticker sheet layout, quantity and packing requirements.

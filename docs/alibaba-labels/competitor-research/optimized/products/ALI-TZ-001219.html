五条卖点

A round face sticker turns an existing blank reel into a branded ID accessory while retaining the customer's chosen reel mechanism.

在既有伸缩扣正面加贴圆形商标，客户可沿用已选扣体。

Sizing the disc to the usable recess keeps its edge inside the rim and helps assemblers center the logo.

按正面凹槽配直径，贴标边缘可收在外圈内，装配时容易找正。

The transparent raised face gives a small emblem a visible edge and a polished surface on a compact accessory.

透明弧面让小标识的轮廓更清楚，在小面积配件上也有立体感。

Several departments can share a cut size while changing the printed color or icon, making assortments easier to specify.

多个部门可共用同一尺寸，通过不同底色或图标区分款式。

Each disc peels off the carrier independently, so the assembler can align an upright logo with the reel clip before pressing.

圆贴可逐枚揭取，按压前能先依照夹子方向把商标摆正。

八组采购 FAQ

Does the price cover the retractable holder?

The supply item is the adhesive domed face sticker. The retractable holder is an application example.

Should I measure the entire reel?

Measure the flat usable front or inner recess, not the outside housing. Include a photo with a scale and the rim depth if recessed.

How can I keep the logo upright?

Decide on the clip orientation first. Use a reference mark or assembly jig to align the artwork before pressing the disc down.

Can each department use its own design?

Yes. Supply a separate artwork file and quantity for each department, together with the shared cut diameter if applicable.

Will it fit a curved reel face?

Fit must be sampled on the actual face. A domed or textured housing can reduce adhesive contact at the edge.

Can you print small lettering?

Supply vector text at the intended size. Review a full-size proof and physical sample to confirm legibility through the dome.

What should I test on a working reel?

Check edge clearance, bonding and access to the clip and cord. Approve a fitted sample before ordering the full run.

What details are needed for a quotation?

Send the usable face diameter, housing material, artwork versions, pieces per version and packing request.

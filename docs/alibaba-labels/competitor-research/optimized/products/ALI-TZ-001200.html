八条产品FAQ

按第1问、第1答至第8答的顺序复制，每次一项。

What is supplied with one piece?

One piece refers to one sticker book in a selected style. The reference book contains 24 sheets; individual sticker motifs are arranged on those sheets.

What material is used for the stickers?

The sticker face material is self adhesive washi paper supported by release backing. The cover and retail bag are separate components.

What are the reference dimensions and sheet count?

The reference sticker sheet size is 170 x 240 mm and the book contains 24 sheets. These are product reference specifications rather than shipping package dimensions.

Are all four styles included in one piece?

No. Like a Dream, Fleeting Years, Floral Splendor in the City and Splendid and Dazzling are separate style options. Specify the required number of books for each style when ordering.

How do I use the stickers?

Peel a precut printed motif from its release backing and press it onto a clean, dry journal, planner, scrapbook or card surface. No additional glue is required.

Can the cover and printed artwork be customized?

Yes. Provide your own artwork and specify the cover design, illustrations, colors, book size and required sheet count for a custom printing quotation.

How is the reference book packed?

The reference retail packaging is a hanging hole OPP bag. Include any outer carton, labeling and packing requirements with your inquiry.

What information is needed for an accurate quotation?

Send the number of books, style quantities, sheet size, sheets per book, artwork, packing requirements and delivery destination. Pricing is calculated per complete book rather than per individual sticker.

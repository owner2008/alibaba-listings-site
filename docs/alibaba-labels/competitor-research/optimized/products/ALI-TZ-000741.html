八条产品FAQ

按第1问、第1答、第2问、第2答的顺序复制，每次只复制当前问题或答案。下方单独复制按钮仍可使用。

What is included in one box?

The illustrated set contains 12 rolls of patterned washi tape in a printed box. Each roll is 15 mm wide and 3 m long.

Can the twelve rolls have different artwork?

Yes. Supply a numbered artwork list from 1 to 12 and indicate the desired box order so each design can be matched to its roll.

How do I prepare the repeat for a 15 mm strip?

Lay out the pattern at finished width, mark the repeat length and preview two repeats side by side. Check that circles and hearts meet cleanly at the join.

How should I identify the gold foil details?

Mark the metallic elements on a separate layer registered to the color design. A physical sample lets you check alignment and reflection on the printed paper.

Can the box match my tape collection?

Supply the box artwork or specify which roll motif to carry onto the box. Review the proposed layout and logo placement on the box proof.

Will it lift cleanly from my planner paper?

Removal depends on the page surface. Test a sample on the actual planner stock and check for lifted fibers or residue before committing to the run.

What should I approve on the sample?

Check the pastel colors, foil alignment and repeat at actual width. Compare the roll assortment and printed box against your numbered design list.

What do you need for a custom quotation?

Send the number of boxed sets, the design list, foil coverage, box artwork and delivery destination. Flag any changes to the illustrated roll size or count.

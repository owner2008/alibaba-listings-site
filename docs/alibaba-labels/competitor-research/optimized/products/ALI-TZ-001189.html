Can I use my own logo and honey design?

Which size should I choose for my honey jar?

How are the labels applied?

Use a clean dry jar surface. Peel the printed label from the release liner, align it on the jar and smooth from the center toward the edges. Evaluate a physical sample on your chosen container before bulk application.

What roll details should I provide?

How are samples and quantity prices quoted?

产品 FAQ

What is included on the business sticker sheet?

The planned configuration combines business logo stickers and coordinated package seals on a shared release liner.

Are the stickers supplied in sheets or rolls?

This listing is for kiss cut sticker sheets. The stickers are peeled individually from a sheet.

Can I include more than one design on a sheet?

Provide the artwork files and the quantity of each design per sheet for layout review.

What can the stickers be applied to?

The planned uses include external retail boxes, gift bags and paper wrapping. Provide the actual surface for selection.

How do I specify the size?

Provide the width and height of each sticker and the preferred outer sheet size in millimetres.

Can I use my own business logo?

Provide artwork that you own or are authorized to use, together with a reference for any required brand colours.

What is the quotation unit?

Request a quotation by finished sheet and specify the number of stickers and designs on each sheet.

What should I send for proofing?

Send the artwork, exact wording, cut outlines, colour reference and package surface before approving the layout.

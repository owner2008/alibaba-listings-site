Is this a self adhesive label or a hang tag?

It is an individual cardboard hang tag with a punched hole. It has no adhesive backing or release liner and is attached using a specified cord configuration.

What is the surface process shown on the logo?

The selected process is flock printing with a fine pile raised graphic. It is distinct from soft touch lamination or mechanical embossing. The images illustrate the intended appearance; a physical sample establishes the final tactile result.

What material and shape are available for this design?

The reference configuration uses cardboard with a custom shape. The images show ivory paperboard clipped upper shoulders and rounded lower corners. Specify the board and die cut requirements with your artwork.

What tag sizes can I specify?

The size image shows 50.8 x 88.9 mm and 50.8 x 101.6 mm width by height as manufacturer published rectangular hang tag examples. These are not measured prototype dimensions or a universal standard. Custom dimensions are quoted to your drawing.

Are cords included with every tag?

Cord inclusion and assembly follow the itemised order configuration. Specify the cord type colour length tag quantity and whether threading is required. Confirm accessory quantities separately from the number of printed tags.

Can I replace the front logo and back information?

Yes. Supply your approved brand artwork and the text for each side. The LN monogram LABELNEXOR COLLECTION 01 and STYLE 001 SIZE M COLOR IVORY shown are demonstration content rather than verified information for your garments.

Where can these hang tags be used?

They are intended for indoor apparel and fashion accessory presentation. Attach through a suitable sewn label loop or other approved attachment point. Check the tag and cord arrangement on the actual item before bulk use.

How do I request a price and production schedule?

What is included with this product?

Custom printed self adhesive vinyl Thank You sticker labels on release liner rolls. The daisy, heart and lettering are printed artwork. Boxes, envelopes and other packaging props are not included.

Can I change the daisy design or add my logo?

Which label sizes are available?

Common round examples are 25.4 mm, 38.1 mm and 50.8 mm diameter. Custom sizes and outlines are available. The size illustration is not to scale; use the stated dimensions when preparing artwork.

How do I apply the stickers?

Use a suitable clean, dry surface. Peel a printed label from the release liner, position it and press from the center toward the edges. Test a sample on your packaging material before bulk application.

Where can these thank you labels be used?

Use them on suitable envelope flaps, gift bags, box seals and tissue wraps for customer orders, handmade goods and gift packaging. Choose the size to match the available flat sealing area.

What are the minimum order and production lead time?

MOQ from 500 pieces. Lead time from 5 days. Send the finished label size, artwork, quantity and roll packing requirements to arrange your order.

How can I get a quotation?

Which label sizes are available?

Common round examples are 25.4 mm, 38.1 mm and 50.8 mm diameter. Custom sizes and outlines are available. The size illustration is not to scale; use the stated dimensions when preparing artwork.

How do I apply the stickers?

Use a suitable clean, dry surface. Peel a printed label from the release liner, position it and press from the center toward the edges. Test a sample on your packaging material before bulk application.

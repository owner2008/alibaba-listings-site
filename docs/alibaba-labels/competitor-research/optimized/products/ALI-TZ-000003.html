八条产品FAQ

按第1问、第1答、第2问、第2答的顺序复制，每次只复制当前问题或答案。

Can gold foil and embossing be used on the same wine label?

Yes. Specify the foil and embossing areas on the artwork so the finish layout can be reviewed before production.

Which paper gives a premium look?

Cream and textured paper are suitable visual options. Select the actual stock from available samples for your artwork and bottle.

Can small text and a barcode be included?

Yes. Supply final text and barcode artwork, and allow a clear high contrast area. Check the actual printed proof and barcode scan before approving a volume run.

Do you accept a print ready PDF?

Yes. Provide a print ready PDF with fonts outlined or supplied, bleed and final dimensions.

Should foil and embossing be on separate PDF layers?

Separate vector layers or spot color masks are preferred. If the file is flattened, send it for artwork preparation review.

Can labels be supplied on rolls or sheets?

Both formats can be specified. For machine application, include the roll core, unwind direction and gap requirements in the inquiry.

Can I request a sample and see it before shipment?

Request a printed sample and ask for pre shipment photos or video in the inquiry. Sample cost and schedule are quoted after the specification is reviewed.

How are quantity pricing and shipping calculated?

Pricing depends on the label size, material, finishes, order quantity, and supply format. Shipping cost and transit time vary by destination and delivery method. We will provide a detailed quotation once the specifications are confirmed.

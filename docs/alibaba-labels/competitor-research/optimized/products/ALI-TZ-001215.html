详情页五条卖点

1

A separate roast-date label lets roasters use the same preprinted pouch across production runs and add the details of each batch at packing.

同一款预印咖啡袋可跨批次使用，包装时再贴本批次的烘焙信息，换批无需更换整袋图稿。

2

Clearly separated Roast Date and Batch fields let customers check when the beans were roasted and give staff a lot reference for follow-up enquiries.

烘焙日期和批号分栏呈现：顾客可直接查看烘焙时间，门店也能凭批号跟进同批产品。

3

A writable paper face lets small roasters enter dates and lot codes by hand, including short runs where the code changes from one batch to the next.

可书写纸面适合手填日期和批号，小批量烘焙即使批批换码，也能沿用同一款标签。

4

Custom field widths leave room for the roaster’s date format and longest lot code, keeping handwritten entries on their own lines.

填写区宽度可按日期格式和最长批号设计，较长编码也有独立空间，手写内容不易串栏。

5

A compact adhesive label can be sized for a flat panel on the filled pouch, keeping batch details visible without covering the main brand artwork.

小幅背胶标签可按装豆后袋面的平整区域定制，让批次信息清楚可见，同时避开正面的品牌主图。

八条产品FAQ

Which information can I add?

Specify your roast date format and batch reference. Mark any additional field on the artwork.

Can I write the date by hand?

State the pen type and request a sample of the proposed paper for a writing trial.

Where should I place the label?

Choose a smooth exterior panel and check the fit on a filled pouch.

Does the order include the bag?

The quoted unit is a printed label. The coffee bag illustrates placement.

How should I size the writing fields?

Send the longest expected date and lot reference together with your preferred marking method.

What artwork should I send?

Send a vector PDF or editable artwork with the target dimensions and mark any special finish or functional area.

Can I review a proof before bulk production?

Request a digital proof or physical sample and include the intended application when confirming the sample plan.

How do I get a quotation?

Send your target quantity and destination, plus these details: label width height and writing area. Pricing and lead time depend on the approved specification.

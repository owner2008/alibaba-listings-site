产品展示

场景与背景
真实小型仓储或零售后场工作台，浅灰背景，只出现一个封好的牛皮色瓦楞库存纸箱作为实物载体。纸箱胶带封口、瓦楞纸纹理和箱体边角清楚可辨，不放第二类包裹。

功能展示
通过已贴纸箱标签表达SKU、数量、批次和条码的按需直接热敏打印用途；卷首揭离处显示标签面材、胶粘层与离型底纸关系。条码与数据必须标注为视觉示例，不宣称可直接扫描投入生产。

What is supplied with this thermal label product?

Blank heat sensitive adhesive labels supplied on rolls for direct thermal printing.

How is the roll constructed?

Heat sensitive adhesive labels are die cut on a release liner and wound around a roll core.

What printing method is required?

Use a compatible direct thermal label printer. Confirm the printer media size and settings before ordering.

How are the printed labels applied to a carton?

Print the required information, peel the label from the liner, and press it onto a clean flat carton surface.

Are the barcode and variable data preprinted?

No. The roll is supplied blank for buyer printing. Barcode values shown in images are examples and production scanability must be verified.

Are the carton and printer included?

No. The carton and printer are application props. The supplied product is the blank thermal label roll.

What specifications are needed to confirm roll compatibility?

Provide label width height shape core diameter roll outside diameter winding direction and printer model for review.

What are the order quantity and lead time?

Order quantity from 50 rolls. Lead time from 5 days.

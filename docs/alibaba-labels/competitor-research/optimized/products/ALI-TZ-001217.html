八组采购 FAQ

Is the badge a metal nameplate?

It is a printed adhesive label with a clear raised resin face. The equipment in the images illustrates placement.

Which dimensions should I send?

Measure the usable flat width and height and note any corner radius. Send a straight-on photo or drawing of the branding area.

Will it bond to my powder coated panel?

Test an adhesive sample on the actual coating. Surface texture and cleaning products can change adhesion.

Can it sit beside a hot outlet?

Choose a cooler flat area. Send the operating temperature and distance from the outlet for a material review before ordering.

Can I use my own brand colors?

Yes. Supply vector logo artwork and a color reference. Review a domed physical proof under your normal display lighting.

Can I use the same badge on several machines?

Compare the smallest available branding area and the surface finish on each model. Approve fit and adhesion on each distinct finish.

What does the assembly team receive?

The proposed supply format is peelable labels on a release liner. Specify pieces per sheet if assembly needs a particular layout.

What sets the price and lead time?

Send finished dimensions, artwork versions, quantity per version, panel material and delivery location for a specific quotation.

供货结构

背标处理

八条产品FAQ

Can gold foil and embossing be combined on one honey jar label?

Yes. The wordmark can be embossed and the honey dipper can receive gold hot stamping. Final registration is checked against the approved artwork and sample.

Is the cream texture part of the synthetic paper?

The proposed label uses a smooth cream synthetic paper appearance with a low contrast printed microtexture. The texture shown in the artwork is a printed effect.

Can the back label include small text and a nutrition panel?

Yes. Supply the final approved text and nutrition data; review a physical print proof to confirm legibility at the selected size.

Can a barcode be added?

Yes. Supply the approved barcode file and leave a clear high contrast area. Scan the printed proof before approving production.

Can I send a print ready PDF?

Yes. Send final dimensions, bleed, outlined fonts, and artwork. Separate vector layers or spot color masks for foil and embossing are preferred.

Can you prepare the foil and embossing layers from my file?

Send the file for artwork review. If the finish masks are missing, the required prepress work can be assessed before sampling.

Are rolls or sheets available?

The sample concept shows printed labels on rolls. Specify roll core, unwind direction, and label gap for machine application, or request sheets for manual labeling.

How are samples, quantity prices, production time and freight quoted?

These are quoted after the finished size, artwork, material, finishes, order quantity, supply format and delivery address are confirmed.

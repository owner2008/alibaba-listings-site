Can the wrap and lid designs match?

Yes. A coordinated design can carry the same brand product name colours and artwork across rectangular wraps and round lid labels. Specify the quantity and artwork version for each label component.

What material and printing are used?

This listing uses thin flexible vinyl self adhesive labels with colour printing and a release liner. The blush pink ivory and botanical artwork shown is a sample printed design.

How are the labels applied?

Clean and dry the suitable jar surface. Peel the printed wrap from its liner align it with the straight label panel then wrap and press it smoothly. Centre the separate round label on the lid and press the surface and edges.

Are the illustrated sizes fixed?

No. The 203.2 x 50.8 mm wrap and 50.8 mm diameter circle are publicly offered market size examples. Custom sizes are available. Provide the actual jar circumference straight panel height intended wrap gap or overlap and usable lid diameter.

Can I replace the English wording and brand?

What are the MOQ lead time and counting unit?

MOQ starts from 500 individual printed labels and lead time starts from 5 days. State wrap and lid label quantities separately. Label count is not the number of rolls or matched jar sets. Order scheduling follows the final artwork dimensions and roll specification.

What details are needed for a quotation?

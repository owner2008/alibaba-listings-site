详情页五条卖点

1

One adhesive strip spans the carton seam and carries its own tear lane, combining the closure and the opening feature in one component.

一条背胶封条同时跨缝封箱并带有撕开通道，把封口和开箱入口做在同一部件上。

2

A projecting pull tab gives the recipient a place to grip without picking at the edge of the seal.

外露拉头留出手指可抓的位置，收件人无需沿着纸箱接缝反复抠找封条边缘。

3

Parallel perforations guide the tear along the strip, giving the recipient an opening path that does not require running a blade along the box seam.

平行易撕线引导纸条沿预设路径打开，收件人可以顺着拉头开箱，减少沿箱缝划刀的需要。

4

The kraft paper face blends with brown corrugated mailers, while a printed arrow stands out exactly where the box should be opened.

牛皮纸面与棕色瓦楞箱色调协调，起撕端的印刷箭头清楚指出开启方向。

5

Custom strip length and tab position let the seal follow the closure of a shallow mailer or a longer parcel carton while keeping the tab within reach.

封条长度和拉头位置可按箱缝定制，浅型邮寄盒或较长快递箱都能把起撕点留在方便抓取的位置。

八条产品FAQ

How do I choose the strip length?

Measure the seam and the bonding area and send a photo of the assembled carton.

Can I customize the pull indicator?

Mark the desired pull-end text or arrow and its direction on the artwork.

Can I use it on coated mailer boxes?

Send a sample or surface description. Bonding and opening should be tested on the actual carton.

Does the quotation include the box?

The quotation covers the closure strips. Provide a carton sample or drawing to review the design.

Does the strip provide tamper evidence?

The proposed construction is for opening. Any tamper-evidence claim requires a separate specification and test.

What artwork should I send?

Send a vector PDF or editable artwork with the target dimensions and mark any special finish or functional area.

Can I review a proof before bulk production?

Request a digital proof or physical sample and include the intended application when confirming the sample plan.

How do I get a quotation?

Send your target quantity and destination, plus these details: closure length bonding area and pull tab by carton. Pricing and lead time depend on the approved specification.

八组采购 FAQ

Does the dome already have the artwork printed on it?

The reference version is clear and unprinted. The pattern shown beneath a dome is a separate paper insert.

What is the diameter?

The reference circle is 1 inch or 25.4 mm across. Check your actual cap recess before choosing it.

Are the caps and paper circles supplied?

This listing covers adhesive dome stickers on a liner. The cap and paper artwork illustrate how to use them.

How do I fit the cover to my artwork?

Cut a paper test circle, center the dome over the design and check the assembly in the recess before making a batch.

Do I have to mix resin?

No mixing is needed for this design. Lift the finished dome by its edge and apply the adhesive side to the insert.

Will my ink look the same under the dome?

Test your printed stock under a sample. Look for a color shift, visible fibers or air pockets before approving the match.

Can I order a different diameter?

Send the actual flat opening and insert diameter. A different cut size needs a separate feasibility and price check.

What information is needed for a quote?

Send dome diameter, number of pieces, desired pieces per liner, packaging and destination. Identify any request for printed inserts separately.

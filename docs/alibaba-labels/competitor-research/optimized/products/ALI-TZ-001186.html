What is included with this product?

Custom printed round PET self adhesive cap labels on release liner sheets. Cosmetic tubes, caps and makeup products shown in the images are application props and are not included.

Can I change the logo, shade code and color?

Yes. Send your logo, product name, shade code, shade name and preferred colors. LABELNEXOR, 002 NUDE, 004 CORAL and 006 ROSE are original artwork examples, not production data for your product.

Which label diameters are available?

Common round examples are 12.7 mm, 19.05 mm and 25.4 mm diameter. Custom sizes are available. Measure the flat cap area and choose a label that fits inside its edge. Size images are not to scale.

How are gold foil and the raised logo specified?

Mark the foil and raised areas in separate artwork layers. Supply the finished size and vector artwork so the finish combination and sample can be reviewed for your design.

How should the labels be applied?

Use a suitable clean, dry flat cap surface. Peel the label from its liner, position it inside the cap edge and press it into place. Test a physical sample on the chosen cap material and coating before bulk application.

Are the labels supplied on sheets?

The illustrated configuration uses printed labels on release liner sheets. Specify sheet dimensions, label spacing and the required layout. One label piece and one sheet are different counting units.

What is the minimum order and production time?

MOQ from 1000 label pieces. Send the artwork, diameter, finish areas, quantity and packing requirements to request a production schedule with your quotation.

How do I request a quotation?

Send your artwork, label dimensions, PET specification, foil and raised areas, total quantity, sheet layout and delivery destination. Product price, samples, tooling and shipping should be listed separately.

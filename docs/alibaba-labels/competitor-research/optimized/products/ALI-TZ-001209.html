产品FAQ

Which binding options are included?

The proposed options are case binding for textured hardcovers and perfect binding for laminated softcovers. Confirm the selected construction for each title.

What is the proposed textbook size?

The reference trim size is A4, 210 x 297 mm. Confirm the final page size before cover and interior layout approval.

Can the interior use color and black and white printing?

Specify color or black and white for each interior section and identify the color pages when requesting a quotation.

What interior paper and page count should I specify?

This project reference uses 80 to 100 gsm interior paper and approximately 100 to 500 pages per title. Final stock, opacity, thickness and page count require project review.

Can the covers and interior content be customized?

Provide the authorized manuscript, images, cover brief and any layout files. The final wording, design, margins and pagination should be approved before printing.

How are digital and physical proofs arranged?

Digital layout review and a physical sample can be agreed before mass production. Sample charges, shipping and timing depend on the approved specification.

How can the books be packed?

The proposed packing method is individual shrink wrap with protective bulk cartons. Confirm the carton loading and protective inserts for the final book weight and transport plan.

What information is needed for a quotation?

Send the number of titles, copies and pages per title, trim size, paper, printing colors, binding, cover finish and delivery postal code. MOQ, price, lead time and quality issue handling will be confirmed in the quotation.

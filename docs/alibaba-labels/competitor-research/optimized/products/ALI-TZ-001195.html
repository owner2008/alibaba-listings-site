八条产品FAQ

按第1问、第1答至第8答的顺序复制，每次一项。

What is supplied?

Finished custom printed self adhesive PET stickers with individual release liners. Gift boxes and other display props shown in the images are application examples and are not included.

What creates the silver and rainbow appearance?

The holographic PET face reflects silver tones and shifting rainbow highlights under different lighting and viewing angles. Black printed artwork provides contrast. The images are AI commercial visualisations.

Which shapes are available?

Round hexagonal and custom contour shapes can be specified. Send the intended outline together with the finished dimensions and artwork.

Can I use my own logo and wording?

Yes. Provide your approved logo text and artwork. LABELNEXOR PREMIUM 01 and custom are demonstration designs and will be replaced with the agreed production artwork.

Are 50 mm and 75 mm fixed sizes?

No. These circle diameters are illustrative size options. Select the finished dimensions from the actual usable area on your packaging. The size illustration is not a production drawing.

How do I apply the stickers?

Clean and dry a suitable smooth surface. Peel the printed sticker from its release liner align it to the intended position and press from the centre towards the edges. Assess fit and adhesion on the actual packaging material.

What are the MOQ lead time and quantity unit?

MOQ starts from 500 individual printed stickers and lead time starts from 5 days. State the quantity for each artwork and size. Pieces refer to stickers rather than rolls boxes or assorted sets.

What information is required for a quotation?

Provide the finished size shape artwork quantity per design packaging surface and packing requirements. Include the delivery destination for a separate freight quotation. Production pricing depends on the final specification.

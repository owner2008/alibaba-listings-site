What is included in the order?

Custom printed paper self adhesive wine labels on a release liner. The illustrated supply format is rolls. Wine, bottles, glasses and other scene props are not included.

Can I customize the brand name and illustration?

Yes. Send your logo, wine name, illustration, vintage and preferred colors. GV, the amphora motif, 2025, 750 ML and 14% ALC/VOL are demonstration artwork; production uses your approved content.

Which label sizes are available?

Custom sizes are available. The size guide shows 76.2 x 101.6 mm and 76.2 x 127 mm, width x height, as common manufacturer examples. They are not fixed order sizes and the illustration is not to scale.

How is the raised spot UV specified?

Supply vector artwork with a separate layer identifying the raised UV areas. Select the paper and review a physical print sample to evaluate registration, gloss and relief on your design.

Can I request paper and printing samples?

Request paper swatches and a raised UV print sample to compare texture and touch. State your preferred paper, finished size, UV areas and delivery address; sample and courier charges are itemized.

Can one order include several wine designs?

Yes. List the finished dimensions and quantity for each design separately. Total pieces across a range and pieces per design are different quotation inputs; roll layout and any tooling are listed by configuration.

What are the minimum order and lead time?

MOQ from 500 label pieces. Lead time from 5 days. Request a production schedule for your artwork, number of designs, quantity and packing requirements. Roll count is calculated from the agreed labels per roll.

How should I specify the bottle application?

Send the bottle material and dimensions, application method and expected storage conditions, including refrigeration or ice bucket use. Test a sample on the actual bottle before bulk production and define the adhesive and paper specification in the order.

What product is supplied?

We supply finished printed self adhesive labels for supplement bottles and jars. Bottles, softgels and other props shown in the artwork are application examples and are not supplied with the labels.

What material is shown in this image set?

This product uses opaque white BOPP film with an adhesive backing and release liner. The printed white face supports the custom colors, logo and information layout for your supplement packaging.

Can I customize the artwork and shape?

Yes. Customize your logo, printed colors, text layout, label dimensions and die cut shape for the intended container. The Vitamin E design shown is a sample layout.

Are the labels supplied in rolls?

Yes, the illustrated white BOPP labels are supplied in rolls. State the number of individual labels, labels per roll, core size and winding direction in the order specification.

Which label size should I choose?

Use the available labeling area on your container. The 76.2 by 50.8 mm and 101.6 by 50.8 mm image examples illustrate possible rectangular layouts; custom sizes are available and the illustrations are not to scale.

Can the label include a barcode and product information?

Yes. Your artwork can include a barcode area and product information panels. Supply the final text and barcode data for your packaging project; the sample barcode in the images is illustrative.

What are the minimum order and lead time?

MOQ from 500 pieces and lead time from 5 days. Piece quantities refer to individual labels rather than rolls. The production schedule is listed with the order specification.

What information is needed for a quotation?

Send the label width and height, material, total label quantity, artwork, finish requirements and roll specifications. The quotation lists the production configuration and applicable charges separately.

What is supplied with this heart design?

Custom printed self adhesive vinyl Thank You sticker labels on release liner rolls. The teal background, coral heart and lettering are printed artwork. Boxes and other packaging props are not included.

Can I personalize the heart design and wording?

Yes. Send your logo, original artwork, preferred message and colors. The example combines handwritten thank you with FOR YOUR ORDER, a coral heart and a teal and ivory palette.

What sizes can I choose?

Common round size examples are 25.4 mm, 38.1 mm and 50.8 mm diameter. Width equals height for each circle. Custom sizes and outlines are available. The size image is an illustration, not a measured sample or production drawing.

How should I apply the label?

Choose a suitable clean, dry surface. Peel the printed label from its release liner, position it and press from the center toward the edges.

Where can I use these labels?

Use them on suitable exterior box surfaces, envelope flaps, gift bags and tissue wraps. Test a sample on the specific packaging material and coating before bulk application.

How are roll quantities specified?

State the total number of labels and your preferred roll packing. Label count per roll, roll core and winding requirements are specified for the order. One label piece and one roll are different units.

What are the minimum order and production lead time?

MOQ from 500 pieces. Lead time from 5 days. Send your artwork, size, quantity and packing requirements to arrange your order.

How do I request a price?

Send your artwork, label dimensions, quantity, finish, roll packing and delivery destination for an itemized quote. Product price, samples and shipping are listed separately.

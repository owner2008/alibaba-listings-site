What is the product supplied?

Finished custom printed round self adhesive labels on release liner rolls. Bottles, jars, cartons and other carriers in application images are display props.

Which label materials are available?

Coated paper, kraft paper, white BOPP, clear BOPP and holographic film are available as alternative facestocks. Choose the material for your artwork, package surface and desired appearance.

Can I customize the printed design?

Yes. The logo, text, print colors and round label diameter can be customized. Labelnexor artwork in the images is a demonstration design.

Which label sizes are shown?

The illustrated round diameters are 25.4 mm, 38.1 mm and 50.8 mm. These are manufacturer market examples, not measurements taken from the reference photo. Custom sizes are available.

Which packaging surfaces can be shown with these labels?

Application examples include paper cartons, paper bags, glass jars, plastic bottles, metal tins and outer packaging pouches. Select the adhesive construction for the actual surface and evaluate a sample before production use.

How are the labels applied and supplied?

Peel a printed label from its release liner, position it on a clean, dry packaging exterior and press it flat. Labels are supplied on rolls, with label count, core diameter and winding direction specified for the order.

Is holographic film the same as an anti counterfeit label?

Holographic film provides a rainbow appearance. This listing describes printed packaging labels and does not include a defined security authentication or tamper evident construction.

What information is needed for a quote?

Send the diameter, material, total label quantity, package surface and artwork, together with roll requirements. MOQ is from 500 pieces and lead time is from 5 days. Prices are quoted for the selected specification.

Holographic film provides a rainbow appearance. This listing describes printed packaging labels and does not include a defined security authentication or tamper evident construction.
